v6.23.2 display frame-boundary commit

Base firmware: v6.23.1 SQW robustness with DS3231 SQW ISR on core 1 and late-only fit filtering.
Controller compatibility: v6.22.3 degraded-sync policy (unchanged).

Firmware-only display changes relative to v6.23.1:
- Exact-second ISR publishes the already-rendered framebuffer as PENDING; it no longer changes the scan-active buffer immediately.
- Core-1 HUB75 refresh task adopts PENDING only between complete scan cycles.
- With two framebuffers, rendering waits until that adoption before recycling the previous scan buffer. This closes the race where the old active buffer could be cleared/repainted while the refresh loop was still scanning it.
- GPIO33 remains the logical countdown-boundary publication marker.
- GPIO16 remains the physical refresh-boundary adoption marker.
- DS3231 discipline, SQW core-1 logic, synchronization, START/degraded policy, network-silent RUNNING, fonts, colors, and countdown arithmetic are unchanged.
